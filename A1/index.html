1.

The first thing that drew my attention was the display images on the right side, despite not having loaded yet. Even while a blank rectangle, they occupy a very large amount of visual space. It is clear they are designed to be the center of attention.

2.

This is my list of discrete actions while interacting with the website:

  • Scroll through portfolio
  • Read first entry
  • Realise purpose of calendar
  • Interact with calendar
  • Continue skim-reading portfolio

3.

The portfolio is clearly the main focus of the webpage as it has the greatest visual area and information density. The calendar is more of a fun addition that reinforces the author's creative identity and supplements the user experience.

4.

My most frequent action was scrolling through the portfolio, with clicking on calendar items as an honourable mention. Ultimately, the functional purpose of this website is showing the author's work, and that shows in the general behaviour of both me and other potential visitors.

5.

The webpage is fundamentally a design portfolio, but it includes cute interactive elements to give it more personality and express the author's passion for design.

6.

The advent calendar doesn't have a functional purpose, but is instead designed to engage users in a short but fun experience of opening each window and playing with the gifts inside.

7.

The portfolio is the main focus of the webpage, with the advent calendar supplementing the reading experience. I think the intended interaction timeline is close to my own experience.

8.

The first portfolio entry serves as the intended way to discover how the calendar works. From there, users will likely play with it for a few moments, then continue scrolling through and reading the rest of the portfolio.

9.

The portfolio features a variety of different media forms, including:

  • Interaction Design
  • Graphic Design
  • Exhibition Design
  • Brand Identity

10.

The first portfolio entry is intentionally placed where it is to explain the calendar to the user at the start of their viewing experience and encourage them to interact with it.

11.

The portfolio entries have incomplete descriptions that generate curiosity for what the remaining content might be. This may prompt users to expand the entry into its full description.

12.

The portfolio entries being expandable isn't quite apparent enough, which made me confused as to why the descriptions were cutting off. A large button would be preferable to a loose implication of interactability in the title tag.

13.

I found the advent calendar to be fairly engaging for how much time you would typically spend with it. The feeling of discovery with opening each window is a small dopamine hit for the human brain.
