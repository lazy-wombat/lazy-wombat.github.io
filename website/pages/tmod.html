Remnants

2022

My largest and most successful mod. Remnants redesigns the entire world map to be more fun and interesting to explore, while adding its own unique content on the side. Because world generation is so challenging in the modding scene, my extensive practice in this field has allowed my mod to truly stand out among its competitors. Since then, Terraria modding has been my favourite hobby, as it lets me fully express my creativity while being recognised for it.


Challenge Vaults

2024

My latest mod. Challenge Vaults adds doors scattered across your world that lead to difficult obstacle courses with unique rewards. Because the scope of this content was a little different from my usual works, I decided to make it in a separate mod, as it would give me the creative freedom I felt I needed to execute it well.
