Animation Project

2024

My animation project was created with Adobe Animate over the course of 4 weeks. The plot follows the journey of an advanced aircraft across a distant exoplanet, with the mission of laying foundations for high-end research. It took me some time to finalise my idea, as I wanted my animation to convey a meaningful story, but also needed it to fit within the time constraint for this project. Using Maya, I decided to 3D model the aircraft, in order to save time drawing assets for each individual scene.


Character Project

2024

My character project was developed over the course of 4 weeks, using no specific software, with the final product being a remote-controlled droid. Doing such a complex design posed significant challenges throughout the project, but I was fortunately able to manage in the long run. Attention to detail is highly important to me, so I still tried giving it the intricacy and functionality of what might be a real-world robot.
